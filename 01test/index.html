Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship refers to the responsible and appropriate use of technology and the internet.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the legal framework that governs the use of technology and the internet.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is a set of rules that outline how technology and the internet should be used appropriately within an organization or institution.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work or ideas without giving them proper credit.

Plagiarism is plagiarism regardless of whether or not it is intentional.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty refers to any behavior that violates the principles of honesty and integrity in an academic setting.

Academic dishonesty can include plagiarism, cheating, and other forms of academic misconduct.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

Rules for AI generated content may vary depending on the context and platform, but generally include guidelines for transparency, attribution, and ethical use of AI technology.

AI is a supplemental tool not intended to replace human judgment or creativity.
